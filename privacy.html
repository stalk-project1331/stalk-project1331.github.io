Privacy Policy

Clear rules for how the website and application handle information.

Your data stays yours

STALK is designed to help users organize investigation data in a secure and structured workspace. We respect the confidentiality of information processed through the application and the website.

  • We do not sell or monetize personal data collected through the app or the download experience.
  • Files, URLs, notes, and investigation data remain under the control of the user unless explicitly shared.
  • The app and website may collect only minimal technical data required to ensure correct downloads, stability, and support.
  • We may update this policy over time, and the latest version will be published on this page.
